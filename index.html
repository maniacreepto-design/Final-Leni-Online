How this works

How fast will I hear back?
NatureInitial replyTarget update
LRP concern (bullying, harassment, GBV, safety threat)Within 24 hoursWithin 5 school days, or immediately if urgent
General concern (facilities, schedule, etc.)Within 3 school daysWithin 10 school days
Suggestion / commendationAcknowledged within 5 school daysConsidered in the next program review
What happens after I submit?
  • Your entry is logged by the Guidance Designate / LRP Advocate and given a reference number.
  • It's sorted as an LRP concern, a general concern, or a suggestion.
  • LRP concerns go straight to the School Head; general concerns go to the right teacher or office; suggestions are considered for future programs.
  • If you shared your name, you'll get a direct response. Anonymous updates may be posted on the bulletin board without naming anyone.
Is this really confidential?
  • Only the Guidance Designate/LRP Advocate and School Head can open the log.
  • Your identity, if you share it, isn't revealed without your consent — unless the law requires it or your safety is at risk.
  • No learner is punished for a good-faith report.
  • Reports of immediate danger are escalated right away, whether or not you're anonymous.
Who's behind this?

Project L.E.N.I. (Learners Encouraged, Nurtured, and Involved) follows DepEd Order No. 006, s. 2026 on Ensuring a Safe and Motivating Learning Environment.
